## Reusable patterns extracted
- Hook starts inside conflict, not setup.
- A concrete object/evidence drives the reveal: phone, photo, document, ring, message.
- Relationship betrayal + hidden truth + reversal is repeatedly represented.
- Strong status/identity reveal creates a second escalation.
- Episodes can work at 43–97s; longer serialized clips also appear as outliers.
- Cliffhanger is the recurring retention device.
- Production target: original characters, original dialogue, original visuals; do not reuse copyrighted footage or copy plot text.
