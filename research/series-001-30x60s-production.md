# Series 001 Production Script — THE ENVELOPE
## EP01–EP10
EP01 — Hook: “Do not marry Adam.” Lina opens the envelope. Adam calls her name from downstairs. He asks: “Did you find it?” Cut.
EP02 — Lina freezes. Adam claims he never entered her room. She asks why he knows about the envelope. He says: “Because I mailed it.” Cut.
EP03 — Lina demands an explanation. Adam says the photo inside is fake. Lina turns it over: tomorrow's date is written on the back. Cut.
EP04 — Lina searches the woman in the photo. The woman is listed as dead eight years ago. Her phone rings. The caller ID shows the dead woman's name. Cut.
EP05 — The caller whispers: “Lina, don't trust the man downstairs.” Lina looks through the window. Adam is already looking up at her. Cut.
EP06 — Lina hides the phone. Adam enters and says the caller knows her. He asks: “Did she mention your childhood?” Lina says no. He smiles. Cut.
EP07 — Lina follows Adam to his apartment. He opens a locked room. Inside are Lina's childhood drawings. Cut.
EP08 — Adam catches her. He says the room belonged to someone else. Lina finds her name on an old box. Cut.
EP09 — The box contains a birth certificate naming a different mother. Lina asks Adam who her real mother is. He says: “The woman who just called you.” Cut.
EP10 — Lina calls the number. A woman answers: “You finally remembered me.” Lina asks why she was abandoned. The woman says: “You weren't abandoned. You were hidden.” Cut.
EP11 — The woman sends a second envelope before Lina can ask another question. It contains a key and one sentence: “Ask Adam what it opens.” Cut.
EP12 — Adam sees the key and goes silent. Lina asks what it opens. He says: “A door your father wanted forgotten.” Cut.
EP13 — The key opens a storage unit. Lina finds a cassette labeled with her childhood nickname. Cut.
EP14 — The cassette plays her father's voice: “If she remembers, they will come back.” A second voice says Lina's name. Cut.
EP15 — Lina recognizes the second voice as Adam's father. Adam admits his father knew her family. Cut.
EP16 — Adam says Lina's family was hiding evidence of a financial crime. Lina asks why she was involved. He says: “Because you were the evidence.” Cut.
EP17 — Lina meets her biological father. He shows her a photograph proving her identity was changed. Cut.
EP18 — He says Adam's family protected her for years. Lina asks why they suddenly stopped. Cut.
EP19 — Adam arrives with a DNA test. He says: “You deserve the truth.” Lina notices the sample label is wrong. Cut.
EP20 — Lina confronts Adam. He admits the test was altered—but says he did it to stop her father. Cut.
EP21 — Lina asks who is lying. Her mother sends a message: “Both of them.” Cut.
EP22 — Lina finds a hidden camera in her apartment. It has been recording for six years. Cut.
EP23 — The footage shows a boy visiting Lina as a child. The boy looks exactly like Adam. Cut.
EP24 — Adam denies knowing him. Lina asks the boy's name. Adam says: “My brother.” Cut.
EP25 — Lina tracks the brother to an abandoned hotel. He says: “Your mother paid me to keep you alive.” Cut.
EP26 — Lina asks who wanted her dead. He points to a wedding photograph. Cut.
EP27 — The photograph shows Adam's father, Lina's father, and Lina's mother together. Someone has scratched out one face. Cut.
EP28 — Lina restores the image digitally. The missing face is her own mother. Cut.
EP29 — Lina returns to the wedding venue. She places every envelope on the table and says: “Now everyone tells the truth.” Adam enters. Cut.
EP30 — Adam looks at Lina and says: “I was never the person you were warned about.” Lina opens the final envelope: “Then why are you still wearing his ring?” Cut to black.
