# Series 001 — THE ENVELOPE
Format: 30 episodes × ~60 seconds, vertical 9:16
Genre: relationship mystery / betrayal / hidden identity
Rule: original story; trend patterns inform structure only.
Core hook: On her wedding morning, Lina receives an envelope addressed in her own handwriting: “Do not marry Adam.”
1. The Envelope — Lina opens the warning; Adam calls from downstairs; cliffhanger: he asks if she found “the envelope.”
2. The Lie — Lina asks how Adam knows; he says the envelope is a prank; a second handwriting sample matches his.
3. The Photo — inside the envelope is a photo of Lina as a child with a woman she does not recognize.
4. The Date — the photo is dated tomorrow, not years ago.
5. The Missing Woman — Lina searches the photo; the woman is officially listed as dead.
6. The Phone — an unknown number sends Lina a live photo from Adam’s apartment.
7. The Room — Lina discovers a locked room in Adam’s home with her childhood drawings.
8. The Name — a drawer contains a birth certificate with Lina’s name and a different mother.
9. The Mother — the woman from the photo calls Lina and says, “You were never supposed to meet Adam.”
10. The Betrayal — Lina learns Adam has been investigating her family for years.
11. The Second Envelope — another envelope predicts Lina’s next decision before she makes it.
12. The Witness — an old neighbor claims Lina was taken from another family as a baby.
13. The Tape — a damaged cassette records Lina’s father saying, “If she remembers, we all lose.”
14. The Accident — Lina discovers her parents survived the accident everyone said killed them.
15. The Debt — Adam reveals her family owes someone a secret debt.
16. The Choice — Lina can expose Adam or follow him to meet the person behind the envelopes.
17. The Meeting — the mysterious person is Lina’s biological father.
18. The Photograph — he shows Lina proof that Adam’s family destroyed her original identity.
19. The Deal — Adam admits he married Lina to protect her, not to betray her.
20. The Test — Lina demands proof; her father offers a DNA test.
21. The Result — the test confirms the family connection, but someone altered the sample.
22. The Camera — Lina finds hidden footage proving someone has followed her since childhood.
23. The Brother — a man appears claiming to be Lina’s brother.
24. The Voice — the brother recognizes the voice that has been leaving the warnings.
25. The Trap — Lina stages a fake escape to identify the sender.
26. The Reveal — the sender is Lina’s mother, alive and hiding.
27. The Truth — her mother reveals Lina was hidden to stop a family inheritance war.
28. The Betrayal — Adam’s father ordered the original disappearance.
29. The Wedding — Lina returns to the wedding venue with every secret exposed.
30. The Last Envelope — Lina opens one final envelope: “The person you trusted most is still lying.” Cut to Adam.
## Production rule
Each episode: 0–3s hook → 3–40s escalating evidence → 40–55s reversal → 55–60s cliffhanger.
No episode is released without real MP4 + media validation + approval + platform receipt + post-publication verification.
