# Short Drama Distribution CRM — 2026-09-28

Canonical commercial-distribution map for original short-drama series. Research date: 2026-09-28.

## Operating rule

Do not treat an app as a buyer merely because it hosts short dramas. Classify each route as:
- DIRECT_PUBLISH_REVENUE_SHARE: creator can publish and monetize directly.
- ACQUISITION_LICENSE_PITCH: finished series/IP is pitched for licensing, commissioning, or distribution.
- DISCOVERY_ONLY: useful for audience acquisition, not a direct episode-sale channel.
- PRODUCTION_TOOL: can help make the series but is not a distribution buyer.

No exclusive rights are granted without a contract review covering territory, term, exclusivity, IP ownership, sequel/remake rights, dubbing/subtitle rights, social clips, revenue waterfall, minimum guarantee, and reversion.

## Required package for buyer/licensing targets

1. One-page pitch.
2. Series bible.
3. Logline + synopsis.
4. Character bible.
5. Episode grid.
6. 3 strongest finished episodes.
7. Full season delivery plan.
8. Trailer.
9. Vertical poster/key art.
10. English subtitles + Arabic subtitles.
11. Rights declaration.
12. Music/voice/asset licenses.
13. AI-production disclosure where required.
14. Audience evidence once published.
15. Clean master files + checksums.

## Release architecture

Direct monetization layer:
Generated Worlds + OpenDrama + Koe + Yoh + Redrama, subject to account/terms eligibility.

Buyer/licensing layer:
ReelShort + DramaBox + NetShort + GoodShort + HOLYWATER/My Drama.

## Evidence rules

- No platform is marked LIVE until account authorization is verified.
- No publication is marked PUBLISHED without platform response/receipt and post-publication verification.
- No revenue rate is treated as contractual unless supported by current binding terms.
- Third-party comparisons are research leads, not legal/commercial terms.
