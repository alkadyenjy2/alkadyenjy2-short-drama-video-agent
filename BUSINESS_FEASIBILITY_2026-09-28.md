# Business Feasibility Audit — 2026-09-28

## Gate
First 6 months: zero-cost/free-first. Paid subscriptions only after revenue or a verified production bottleneck.

## Product thesis
Short-drama production automation: trend research → story adaptation → episode planning → continuity → scene/visual → voice/SFX → render → media QA → publishing → analytics.

## Market evidence
Grand View Research estimates the global creator economy at $310.4B in 2026, with video streaming the largest platform segment in 2025 and a 23.3% projected CAGR through 2033. MEA creator economy revenue was estimated at $27.4B in 2025. Sources: https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/creator-economy-market-report ; https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/creator-economy-market/mea

## Customer / GTM
Initial ICP: owned short-drama creator channel and later other creators/publishers. Zero-cost GTM: organic Shorts/TikTok/YouTube publishing and repurposing existing assets. No paid media until organic proof.

## Monetization hypothesis
Platform ad/revenue share, licensing, sponsorships, and later creator-service revenue. None should be treated as guaranteed.

## Economics / timing
The critical cost risk is media generation/rendering. Current pipeline has not yet proven reliable acted-video output; a static image is not acceptable as an episode. Paid generation must wait for revenue or a verified blocker.

## Decision gate
VALIDATE BEFORE PAID BUILD: prove one real playable acted episode with verified video+audio streams, then test organic audience response.

## Builder contract
No fake video, fake audio, fake publish receipt, or static-image substitution. Media QA must verify playable MP4, duration, resolution, video stream and audio stream.
